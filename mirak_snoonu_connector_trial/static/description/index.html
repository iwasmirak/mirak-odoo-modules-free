Prices that match your pricelists

The price comes from the Sales Price or from any Odoo pricelist, converted to the store currency. When the pricelist reduces the price, the reduction is sent as a Snoonu discount amount so the promotion shows on the storefront.

Stock per branch, never oversold

Each Snoonu branch is linked to an Odoo warehouse and its own stock rule: available, on hand or forecasted, with a safety buffer per branch. A sale in Odoo or in Snoonu is reflected on the next synchronization.

Only what changed is sent

Every SKU carries a fingerprint of its last payload. The connector compares and sends only the products whose price, discount, stock or visibility changed, in batches, with retries on temporary errors.

Products sent, waiting, in error, last synchronization and the exact payload that was transmitted — per account.

Accounts and branches

  • Several Snoonu accounts (brands / business units) in the same database.
  • Staging and production environments side by side, with their own URLs.
  • Branches linked to Odoo warehouses, with brand-level prices or branch prices.
  • Multi-company: every account belongs to a company and its rules follow it.

Pricing and discounts

  • Price from the Sales Price or from a pricelist, with the store currency.
  • Pricelist reduction sent as a Snoonu discount amount (Snoonu never takes percentages).
  • Brand price and branch price sent in the same request, with UseDefaults per branch.
  • Rounding follows the currency precision, so the storefront shows clean amounts.

Stock

  • Available, on hand or forecasted quantity, chosen per account.
  • A safety buffer per branch, subtracted from the published stock.
  • Several warehouses, several branches, one request.
  • Stock is re-read after a move in the same transaction, so a sale is never missed.

Visibility

  • Hide a product on Snoonu without touching its stock (IsAvailable).
  • Products archived in Odoo are hidden automatically.
  • A product that is not sellable in Odoo is never published.

Reliability

  • Delta synchronization based on a hash of the last payload.
  • Batches of up to 500 products, configurable per account.
  • Three attempts with back-off on 429 / 5xx, then a clear error on the product.
  • Detailed log of every request and response, with the HTTP status and the duration.

Comfort

  • A preview of the exact JSON payload before sending anything.
  • A test button that sends one sample product to the sandbox.
  • Manual synchronization per product, per selection or for the whole catalogue.
  • Automatic synchronization every N minutes, switchable per account.

Requirements

  • Odoo 17, 18, 19 or 20 — Community or Enterprise, with Sales and Inventory.
  • A Snoonu merchant account with API access (registered email + API key).
  • The business unit ID given by Snoonu, and the production URL once staging is validated.
  • The Python package `requests` (already installed with Odoo).
