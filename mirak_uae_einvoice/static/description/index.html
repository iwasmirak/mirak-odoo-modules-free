Official documents, rich mappings

Tax invoices, out-of-scope invoices and credit notes include UAE VAT categories, transaction flags, AED totals, exchange rates, payment means, delivery details and item identifiers.

Errors stopped before sending

Business checks, UBL XSD and optional official Schematron validation produce clear English or Arabic messages with rule references. A document with errors is never sent.

A complete operational flow

Statuses, retries, rejection handling, chatter, mass sending, vendor bill import, readiness scoring and a dashboard keep the mandate manageable day to day.

Readiness and document status at a glance

PINT-AE generation

  • Document types 380, 480, 381 and 81.
  • VAT categories S, Z, E, O and AE reverse charge.
  • Line gross prices, discounts, allowances and VAT-inclusive amounts.
  • Foreign-currency exchange rate and AED VAT totals.

Validation and exchange

  • Own pre-checks plus shipped OASIS UBL 2.1 XSD.
  • Optional SaxonC-based official Schematron validation.
  • Configurable REST connector for an Accredited Service Provider.
  • Simulation accept, reject, alternate and error behaviours.

Receiving and controls

  • Import vendor PINT-AE files as Odoo vendor bills.
  • Supplier matching by TRN, Peppol ID or name.
  • Product, purchase-tax, allowance, charge and total mapping.
  • Duplicate UUIDs refused and sent invoices protected from draft reset.

Security and usability

  • Provider API keys never written to logs or exchange messages.
  • English and Arabic right-to-left interface.
  • Multi-company record rules and user/administrator groups.
  • Readiness checker with weighted score and direct Fix actions.

Requirements

  • Odoo 17, 18, 19 or 20 — Community or Enterprise, with Accounting.
  • Python `lxml` and `requests`; optional `saxonche` for official Schematron checks.
  • A contract with an Accredited Service Provider for live Peppol/FTA exchange (full edition).
  • The module itself is not an Accredited Service Provider.
